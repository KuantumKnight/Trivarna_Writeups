Saffron Glitch Badge capture notes
==================================

Oscilloscope export columns:

time_ns,vcc_mV,trigger,reset_n,debug

Capture settings:

- nominal VCC: 3300 mV
- sample interval: 250 ns
- trigger rises once per firmware cycle
- reset_n is briefly pulled low before the useful capture window
- debug is a slow timing pin, not a serial UART

Bench reminder:

The badge was built for the 15-08-1947 ceremony test.
The real signal is in the power rail after reset, not in the readable-looking debug pulses.
